title: Don't adjudicate pixel/animation questions with a VLM read — go to the artifact + controlled render
scenario: "Reviewing a generated animated SVG (pelican-bike.svg). A read_image pass on a 2x2 frame montage told me \"the background never moves, no parallax, scene is locked, reads as pedalling on a treadmill\", while my own pixel diff said 21-27% of pixels (51.6% of the tree-line band) change between panels. I escalated the dispute to the VLM twice more (a stacked treeline crop and an amplified diff heatmap) and both reads were unreliable: one invented \"the dog's position\" and \"a red bike frame\" in a pelican scene, the other claimed the background was \"black\" while the heatmap is bright across the full panel width, and the two reads contradicted each other."
root_cause: "Treating a generative vision read as evidence about pixel-identity, and using a VLM to adjudicate a question that is decidable by exact arithmetic and by the artifact itself. VLMs re-describe imagery fluently rather than comparing pixels, so they cannot certify identity/inequality between frames, and they will invent plausible objects to fill gaps."
solution: |
  Escalate identity/difference claims to the artifact, not to a second VLM. Concretely, for "does the background move?": (1) inventory every animation element in the source and print each one's ancestor chain, so parent-targeted SMIL (animateTransform with no href = animates its parent group) is attributed to the right layer; (2) freeze the SMIL at two instants by rewriting each layer group's transform to the interpolated offset and rasterise both, then cross-correlate to recover the actual pixel shift; (3) cross-check against the generator's own invariants. Here all three agreed: 9 top-level layer groups carry dur=6s translate animations at 6 distinct distances, gen.py asserts the ground scroll equals wheel rolling distance, and the frozen re-render at t=0 vs t=0.30s matched the right-hand tree band at dx=-130px (SAD 4.08 -> 0.69) and the ground at |dx|=376 (SAD 9.60 -> 3.37) while the character stayed fixed. A 6x SAD drop at one specific shift cannot be a hallucination, so the original "static background" review was wrong and the animation was sound. Also: prefer the control group - if freezing the background at t=0 for all panels still yields inter-panel diffs, the diffs are real scene change, not resampling.
  
  CURRENTNESS CHECK (re-run before reusing): cd /Users/yyj/Project/llm-first-loop-mirror/outputs/pelican-bike-20261017 && grep -c 'animateTransform' pelican-bike.svg && grep -n 'SCROLL_NEAR' gen.py && grep -o 'values="0 0;-[0-9]* 0"' pelican-bike.svg | sort | uniq -c
    -> expect 9 parent-targeted dur="6s" translate layers, SCROLL_NEAR = 6600.0, and the distance set -1320 -2640 -3960 -6600 -15840 -11880. If a count differs, the layer layout changed and the dx figures must be re-measured, not reused.
